I'm also the founder of **[Clearbound Consulting](https://clearboundconsulting.com)**, where I work directly with organizations on technology strategy, software architecture, custom software, AI & automation, and team enablement.

I like taking complicated problems and turning them into systems that are practical, maintainable, and understandable.

## 🎓 Educator + Engineer

One of the things I care most about is closing the gap between **learning software development** and **practicing software engineering**.

That means giving students opportunities to work with the same concepts they will encounter professionally:

`Git` · `Pull Requests` · `Code Reviews` · `Issues` · `Testing` · `CI/CD` · `Architecture` · `Documentation` · `Production Releases`

I believe students learn software engineering best when they have the opportunity to **build real things, for real users, as part of a real team**.
